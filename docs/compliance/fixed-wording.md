## Regulatory precision

- The rapid-drain safeguard applies to personal accounts at the retail business of the seven D-SIBs;
  implementation varies by bank; all must meet SRF section 4.2.5.
- The 12-hour cooling-off (capability friction after high-risk account events) and the rapid-drain 24-hour hold
  (transaction flow) are two different mechanisms.
- A policy outcome is shown as a fact, never as a probability or confidence figure.
